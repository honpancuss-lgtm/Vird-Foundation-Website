WHY VOLUNTEER

Give your time and skills

VIRD Foundation welcomes volunteers with skills and experience in areas including community development, health, nutrition, education, agriculture, WASH, finance, ICT, communications, research, monitoring and evaluation, fundraising, events, administration and youth development.

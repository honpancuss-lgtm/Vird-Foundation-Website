SHARIA ADVISORY COUNCIL

The Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation establishes the Sharia Advisory Council as an independent advisory and Sharia-review organ to provide qualified guidance and assurance concerning Islamic community-development, charitable and social-finance activities.

Purpose

  1. Provide independent Sharia guidance to the Board and management.
  2. Review and advise on the design and implementation of Islamic charitable and social-finance instruments.
  3. Protect the integrity of restricted Islamic funds and faith-specific programmes.
  4. Promote consistent, documented and auditable Sharia decision-making.
  5. Review material Sharia non-compliance and recommend corrective treatment.
  6. Strengthen confidence among donors, beneficiaries, partners and other stakeholders.

Independent Guidance

Supporting qualified Sharia guidance, integrity, assurance and responsible decision-making across VIRD Foundation's Islamic community-development, charitable and social-finance activities.

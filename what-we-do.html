PROGRAMME 09

Islamic Social Finance & Philanthropy

Turning Charity into Sustainable Opportunity

VIRD foundation seeks to responsibly mobilize and manage Islamic social finance instruments to support vulnerable individuals and communities. These may include:

Zakat

Supporting eligible vulnerable people and households in accordance with applicable requirements and appropriate Islamic guidance

Sadaqah

Mobilizing voluntary charitable contributions for approved humanitarian and development purposes.

Waqf

Supporting sustainable community assets and services through responsible endowment arrangements

Qard Hasan

Where appropriately approved and structured, VIRD foundation may facilitate interest-free financing mechanisms to support eligible beneficiaries.

Ramadan Giving: Mobilization and distribution of charitable assistance during Ramadan to support vulnerable individuals and households with essential needs and enable them to observe the holy month with dignity.

Eid Assistance: Provision of food, clothing, essential items or financial support to vulnerable families, children and individuals so they can celebrate Eid with dignity and inclusion.

Food Assistance: Provision of food packages, meals or other appropriate food support to food-insecure and vulnerable households, particularly during emergencies, Ramadan and other periods of heightened need.

Vulnerable Household Support: Targeted assistance to households facing severe poverty, disability, displacement, illness, food insecurity or other significant vulnerabilities, based on transparent needs assessment.

Orphan and Child Support: Support for orphans and vulnerable children through education, nutrition, healthcare, protection, essential needs and other services that promote their safety, dignity and well-being.

Livelihood Assistance: Support that helps vulnerable individuals and households develop sustainable income-generating capacity through skills training, productive assets, business support, agriculture, entrepreneurship or appropriate financing.

Sadaqah Jariyah

Developing charitable assets capable of generating continuing community benefit.Examples may include:

  • Water facilities
  • Mosques
  • Qur'an schools
  • Libraries
  • Learning spaces
  • Health facilities
  • Productive community assets

Restricted Islamic funds will be appropriately tracked, managed and reported according to their purpose and applicable requirements.

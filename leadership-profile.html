Advisory Council

Prof. Muhammed Abdulai

Advisory Council Member

Prof. Muhammed Abdulai is an Associate Professor of Sociology and Vice-Dean of the Faculty of Communication and Media Studies at the University for Development Studies (UDS), Ghana. He is an academic leader, researcher and international development consultant with more than a decade of experience in cultural and social change communication, migration and mobility, Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI), safeguarding, social protection, research, monitoring and evaluation, and peace and security programming.

He holds a PhD in Sociology from Tallinn University, Estonia, with specialist focus in cultural communication, gender equality, social inclusion, safeguarding and migration. He has led and contributed to research, evaluation, training and policy assignments for institutions including UNDP Ghana, ODI Global, World University Service of Canada (WUSC) and SNV Ghana. As a VIRD Foundation Advisory Council Member, he contributes expertise in inclusive programme design, evidence generation, policy analysis, institutional strengthening and strategic partnerships.

Core Expertise

Social & Behavioural Change Communication • Migration & Mobility • GESI & Safeguarding • Social Protection • Peace & Security • Research, MEL & Policy Analysis

Mr. John Eliasu Mahama

Advisory Council Member

Dr. John Eliasu Mahama is a public health and development practitioner with over two decades of experience in HIV and AIDS response, programme management and public policy. He serves as Acting Director, Policy and Planning at the Ghana AIDS Commission, where he leads the development of evidence-informed HIV policies and strategic plans and supports national efforts to address human-rights barriers, stigma and discrimination and equitable access to HIV services.

His experience spans development policy, multi-stakeholder engagement, systems strengthening, partnerships and resource mobilisation across Ghana, Senegal, Nigeria and Mali. Between 2023 and 2025, he served on the WHO Advisory Group on Global Prototype Curricula for Community Health Workers and previously worked as Health Advisor with the Millennium Promise Alliance in West Africa. He holds an MSc in International Health from University College London and a Master of Research and Public Policy from the University of Ghana. He is a Member of the Institute of Directors Ghana and a Fellow of the Royal Society of Public Health. As a VIRD Foundation Advisory Council Member, he contributes expertise in inclusive programme design, evidence generation, policy analysis, institutional strengthening and strategic partnerships.

As a VIRD Foundation Advisory Council Member, he contributes expertise in Public Health programming, inclusive programme design, evidence generation, policy analysis, institutional and health systems strengthening as well as strategic partnerships.

Core Expertise

Public Health • Health Policy & Planning • HIV/AIDS Programming • Health Systems Strengthening • Partnerships • Resource Mobilisation • Multi-Stakeholder Engagement

Prof. Abukari Wumbei

Advisory Council Member

Prof. Abukari Wumbei is an Environmental Scientist, academic, researcher and public-sector leader with over 20 years of professional experience in environmental management, Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH), sustainable development, climate resilience and environmental policy. He currently serves as Director of Sanitation at Ghana's Community Water and Sanitation Agency (CWSA), where he provides strategic leadership in advancing safe, sustainable and inclusive sanitation services.

He is an Associate Professor of Applied Biological Sciences and former Director of the Drylands Research Institute at the University for Development Studies (UDS). He holds a PhD in Applied Biological Sciences (Environmental Technology) from Ghent University, Belgium. As a VIRD Foundation Advisory Council Member, he contributes scientific expertise, evidence-based policy insight and strategic leadership to strengthen WASH, climate resilience, environmental sustainability and inclusive community development programming.

Core Expertise

WASH & Sanitation Governance • Environmental Management & EIA • Climate Resilience • Sustainable Resource Management • Food Security • Institutional Leadership

Madam Suhuyini Nawaratu Karmil

Advisory Council Member

Madam Suhuyini Nawaratu Karmil is a Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI) and development specialist with over 10 years of experience across Ghana, Africa and international development. Her expertise spans gender equality and social inclusion, climate justice, agriculture and food systems, governance, social protection, education, economic empowerment, inclusive finance, safeguarding, monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEAL), research, advocacy and institutional strengthening.

She serves as National Gender and Development Specialist with the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO), supporting the integration of gender equality and social inclusion into climate-resilient agriculture, food systems and climate-finance programming. Her professional experience includes work with organisations and programmes such as Right To Play, DAI Global/USAID Feed the Future PolicyLINK, Norsaac, CARE International Ghana, Plan International Ghana and IFDC/SNV 2SCALE, alongside consultancy assignments supporting national and international development initiatives.

She holds an MSc in Development Management from the Institute of Local Government Studies, Ghana, and a BSc in Renewable Natural Resources from the University for Development Studies. As a member of the VIRD Foundation Advisory Council, she contributes expertise in gender-responsive and inclusive programming, climate resilience, livelihoods, social protection, research, partnerships and institutional capacity strengthening.

Core Expertise

GESI & Safeguarding • Climate Justice & Resilience • Agriculture & Food Systems • Social Protection • Economic Empowerment & Inclusive Finance • MEAL, Research & Advocacy • Institutional Strengthening

Board of Directors

Ms. Ramatu Pamboba

Board of Directors Member - Chairperson

BRIEF PROFILE

Ms. Ramatu Pamboba is a senior Ghanaian education professional, school administrator and community-oriented leader with more than three decades of experience in teaching and educational management. Her career within the Ghana Education Service spans classroom teaching, secondary and basic education, school leadership, staff supervision and school-community engagement across Northern Ghana.

She currently holds the rank of Deputy Director of the Ghana Education Service and has served as Headteacher of Hillaliyatul Islamic JHS, Tamale, since 2019. Her earlier leadership appointments include Headteacher of Choggu Nuril Islamic JHS, Wayamba R/C JHS and St. Augustin JHS. Before moving into school leadership, she served as a teacher at Adakura JHS, Zion JHS, Islamic SHS, Kalpohin SHS and Kanvilli R/C JHS.

Ms. Pamboba holds a Bachelor of Education in Home Economics from the University of Education, Winneba, a Teacher Certificate 'A' from Tamale College of Education, and professional training in areas including teacher quality, student performance and Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH). Her professional strengths include interpersonal communication, teamwork, critical thinking, flexibility, resilience and the ability to work effectively with limited resources.

BOARD CONTRIBUTION

As Chairperson of the Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation, Ms. Pamboba provides leadership to the Foundation's principal governing and fiduciary body. She guides Board deliberations, supports effective governance and strategic oversight, and helps ensure that the Foundation remains accountable to its mission, approved strategies, legal obligations, partners and the communities it is established to serve.

Her extensive background in education and school administration strengthens the Board's oversight of education and human-capital development, child and youth-focused programming, community participation and institutional capacity. Her experience working within public education systems also contributes practical insight into leadership, staff management, stakeholder engagement, healthy learning environments and the realities of programme delivery in Northern Ghana.

As Chairperson, she promotes constructive Board engagement, sound decision-making, responsible stewardship, risk awareness and appropriate oversight of Executive Management while respecting the distinction between governance and day-to-day operations. She also supports VIRD Foundation's commitment to inclusive, community-centred and sustainable humanitarian and development programming.

CORE EXPERTISE

Board Governance & Strategic Oversight • Education Leadership & School Administration • Human Capital Development • Child & Youth Development • Community Engagement • Institutional Accountability • Team Leadership & Staff Management • WASH & Healthy Learning Environments • Stakeholder Communication • Northern Ghana Context

Jane Akanyogbasui Anyuisah

Member & Secretary, Board of Directors | VIRD Foundation

Jane Akanyogbasui Anyuisah is a public health and maternal healthcare professional with over a decade of experience serving women, children and communities through the Ghana Health Service. Her professional background spans public health nursing, midwifery and community health nursing, with practical experience in maternal and newborn care, reproductive and child health services, health education, supportive supervision, reporting and data validation.

She currently serves as a Public Health Nurse in Karaga, Ghana, where she coordinates reproductive and child health activities, conducts supportive supervision and coaching visits, and contributes to evidence-based service delivery through regular reporting. Her earlier roles as a Midwife and Community Health Nurse strengthened her expertise in prenatal, antenatal and postnatal care, labour and delivery support, immunization, family planning, community outreach and patient counselling.

Jane holds a Master of Public Health Nursing from the Catholic University of Ghana, a Bachelor of Science in Public Health Nursing, a Diploma in Midwifery, and a Certificate in Community Health Nursing. Her blend of clinical experience, public health practice and community engagement equips her to support VIRD Foundation’s mission of improving the well-being and resilience of vulnerable individuals and communities.

Board Contribution

As Member and Secretary of the Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation, Jane contributes practical expertise in public health programming, maternal and child health, community-based service delivery, monitoring and reporting, stakeholder communication and administrative coordination. Her experience in documentation and supportive supervision strengthens effective board record-keeping, meeting coordination and informed decision-making.

Core Expertise

Maternal, Newborn and Reproductive Health | Public Health Nursing and Community Health | Prenatal, Antenatal and Postnatal Care | Labour and Delivery Support | Patient Education and Counselling | Supportive Supervision and Team Collaboration | Monitoring, Reporting and Data Validation | Board Administration and Documentation Support

Mr. Jamani Kabiru

Member & Treasurer, Board of Directors | VIRD Foundation

Mr. Jamani Kabiru (CA) is a professionally qualified accountant and finance professional with extensive experience in accounting, financial management, public-sector finance, institutional financial administration and governance. He holds an MBA in Finance, a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) and a Higher National Diploma (HND) in Accounting, and is a member of the Institute of Chartered Accountants, Ghana (ICAG).

He has built a distinguished career in Ghana’s public health sector, serving as Head of Finance at Chereponi Government Hospital, Bimbilla Municipal Hospital and Northern Regional Hospital. Across these roles, he has provided leadership in financial planning and management, budgeting, financial reporting, revenue administration, expenditure control, internal controls and institutional accountability.

Mr. Kabiru also brings strong governance and audit oversight experience. He served as a Member of the Audit Committee of Zabzugu District Assembly from 2018 to 2022 and as Chairman of the Audit Committee of Chereponi District Assembly from 2022 to 2026. He is also the Proprietor of New Era Academy in Sagnarigu-Dungu, Tamale, contributing to quality basic education and youth development.

Board Contribution

As Member and Treasurer of the Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation, Mr. Kabiru contributes professional oversight in accounting, budgeting, financial reporting, internal controls, audit, risk management and resource stewardship. His experience supports the Foundation’s commitment to financial discipline, transparency, accountability, sound governance and responsible management of donor and institutional resources.

Core Expertise

• Accounting & Financial Management • Budgeting & Financial Reporting • Public-Sector Finance & Administration • Internal Controls & Compliance • Audit Oversight & Governance • Revenue & Expenditure Management • Institutional Accountability & Resource Stewardship • Education Management & Community Development

Mr. Mustapha Amadu

Board of Directors Member

BRIEF PROFILE

Mr. Mustapha Amadu is a development communication, public relations and local-government professional with extensive experience in public engagement, stakeholder relations, community mobilization and institutional communication. He currently serves as Public Relations Officer and Client Service Manager at the Tamale Metropolitan Assembly, where his responsibilities include advising management, coordinating public and community outreach programmes, engaging the media and serving as a liaison between the Assembly and the public.

In his current public-service role, he also coordinates the sister-city relationship between Tamale and Münster, Germany, and serves as Secretary to the Assembly's Public Relations and Complaints Committee. These responsibilities have strengthened his experience in institutional representation, partnership coordination, public accountability, citizen engagement and complaints management.

Before his current role, Mr. Amadu served as Municipal Information Officer with the Savelugu Municipal Assembly and the Nanumba North Municipal Assembly. In these positions, he supported public education on government policies and programmes, media engagement, citizen feedback, development communication and institutional reporting. His earlier professional experience includes teaching at E.P. College of Education, Bimbilla, where he also served as Head of the Science Department, and at Wulensi Senior High School.

He holds a Master of Arts in Development Education (Community Development) from the University for Development Studies and a Bachelor of Education in Primary Education (Integrated Science) from the University of Cape Coast. His professional strengths include public speaking, communication planning, advocacy, behavioural change communication, stakeholder engagement, community mobilization, facilitation and relationship management.

DEVELOPMENT & COMMUNITY ENGAGEMENT EXPERIENCE

Mr. Amadu has contributed to development-focused initiatives including the Urban Sanitation Project in the Tamale Metropolis, a collaboration involving UNICEF, the Tamale Metropolitan Assembly and the Dutch Government. In that initiative, he participated in implementing a behavioural change communication plan addressing open defecation and promoting household latrine construction. He also led the development and delivery of revenue-mobilization campaign messages across the metropolis in 2021.

BOARD CONTRIBUTION

As a Member of the Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation, Mr. Amadu contributes practical experience in local governance, strategic communication, community engagement and stakeholder coordination. His professional background strengthens the Board's capacity to oversee public accountability, institutional communication, community-centred programming and relationships with government, communities, development partners and the media.

His combination of local-government practice, development communication and educational leadership supports the Foundation in translating governance decisions into clear public communication, constructive stakeholder engagement and stronger community ownership. He also contributes insight relevant to behaviour change, citizen feedback, partnership development, sanitation and community development programming.

Core Expertise

Local Governance & Public Administration • Strategic Communication & Public Relations • Community Mobilization • Stakeholder Engagement • Development Communication • Behavioural Change Communication • Media Relations • Advocacy & Facilitation • Partnership Coordination • Community Development

Prof. Muhammed Abdulai

Board of Directors Member

Associate Professor Muhammed Abdulai is an academic leader, sociologist and international development consultant with more than a decade of experience spanning cultural communication, migration and mobility, social and behavioural change communication, Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI), safeguarding, social protection, research and policy analysis. He currently serves as Vice-Dean of the Faculty of Communication and Media Studies at the University for Development Studies (UDS), Ghana, and as an Associate Professor in Sociology with specialization in Cultural Communication, Gender Equality, Social Inclusion and Migration.

His academic and professional work combines research, institutional leadership and development practice. He has undertaken donor-funded research, evaluation, training and policy assignments for organisations including UNDP Ghana, ODI Global, World University Service of Canada (WUSC), SNV Ghana, and other development partners. His technical expertise includes mixed-methods research, monitoring, evaluation and learning (MEL), stakeholder engagement, inclusive programme design, safeguarding, community resilience, peace and violent-extremism prevention, and capacity strengthening.

Professor Abdulai holds a PhD in Sociology from Tallinn University, Estonia; an MA in Culture, Communication and Globalization from Aalborg University, Denmark; an MA in Cultural Studies from the University of Southeastern Norway; and a BA in Philosophy and Religions from the University of Ghana. He has an extensive record of postgraduate supervision, peer-reviewed publication and international academic engagement, with work published by leading academic publishers including Elsevier, Springer Nature, Taylor & Francis, Sage, Wiley and Palgrave Macmillan.

Board Contribution

As a member of the Board of Directors of VIRD Foundation, Professor Abdulai brings expertise in governance, research and evidence generation, GESI and safeguarding, programme quality, policy engagement, partnership development and institutional capacity strengthening. His experience supports the Foundation's commitment to accountable, inclusive and evidence-informed humanitarian and sustainable development programming.

Mr. Alhassan Hudu Naporo

Board of Directors Member

Mr. Alhassan Hudu Naporo is a public health, environmental and education professional with multidisciplinary experience spanning environmental management, public health, science education, research support, community engagement and institutional leadership. He currently serves as a Programs Officer with Ghana's Environmental Protection Authority, where his responsibilities include environmental surveillance, investigations and verification, technical proposal preparation for resource mobilisation, environmental-impact data collection, stakeholder collaboration, reporting and budgeting.

He holds a Master of Public Health in Epidemiology and Biostatistics from the University for Development Studies, a Bachelor of Science in Public Health and Allied Science (Health Education), a Bachelor of Science in Biology Education and a Diploma in Basic Education. His professional background includes service with the Ghana Education Service, part-time tutoring at the Tamale School of Hygiene and public-health work. He has also held leadership and financial-management roles in national and community organisations. As a VIRD Foundation Board Member, he brings strengths in public health, environmental sustainability, education, community mobilisation, resource mobilisation, organisational leadership and financial stewardship.

Core Expertise

Public Health & Epidemiology • Environmental Management • Science Education • Community Mobilisation • Resource Mobilisation • Organisational Leadership & Financial Management

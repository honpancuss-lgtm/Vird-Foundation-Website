PARTNER WITH VIRD FOUNDATION

Let's build what lasts.

Whether you are a government institution, donor, foundation, corporation, Islamic organization, philanthropist, academic institution or community organization, there is an opportunity to work together.

Ways to partner

Funding, co-financing, technical assistance, community projects, research, training, infrastructure, equipment, Islamic social investment, CSR, volunteerism and knowledge sharing.

Partnership principles

Transparency, accountability, professionalism, community ownership, evidence-based programming, safeguarding and sustainability.

Giving with purpose

Zakat supports eligible beneficiaries through appropriately managed resources; Sadaqah supports humanitarian and development needs; Waqf supports sustainable charitable assets; BARAKA and community sponsorship support integrated transformation; general donations support eligible priorities.

Where We Work

Starting in Northern Ghana, with a deliberate, honest path toward wider reach.

VIRD foundation's initial programming focus is Northern Ghana, with an initial catchment encompassing:

  • Northern Region
  • Savannah Region
  • North East Region
  • Upper East Region
  • Upper West Region
  • Oti Region
  • Bono East Region

The Foundation intends to expand progressively based on:

  • Demonstration need
  • Organizational capacity
  • Evidence
  • Partnerships
  • Funding
  • Security
  • Governance readiness
  • Programme quality

Quality Before Scale

PHASE 1 — CURRENT FOCUS

Northern Ghana

VIRD foundation may prioritise vulnerable communities and districts across the following regions, expanding as capacity and partnerships allow.

  • Northern Region
  • Savannah Region
  • North East Region
  • Upper East Region
  • Upper West Region
  • Bono East Region
  • Oti Region
  • Other regions of Ghana
  • International locations where legally and operationally feasible

Geographic expansion shall be based on organizational capacity, needs, partnerships, resources and Board-approved strategy.

OUR GROWTH PATH

An honest, staged plan for reach

VIRD Foundation is a young organization. We would rather show our real, staged plan than overstate our current footprint.

PHASE 1 — NOW

Northern Ghana

Establishing programmes and community partnerships across our initial focus districts.

PHASE 2

Ghana-Wide Replication

Extending proven models to additional regions of Ghana as capacity and funding allow.

PHASE 3

West Africa & Beyond

International expansion subject to appropriate legal presence, partnerships, qualified personnel, sustainable financing and demonstrated programme capacity. Not yet underway.

HOW WE CHOOSE COMMUNITIES

A transparent basis for expansion

Verified Need

Communities are prioritized based on documented humanitarian and development need.

Community Consent

Programmes begin with community engagement and agreement, not imposition.

Delivery Capacity

Expansion follows VIRD Foundation's actual capacity to deliver and stay accountable.

COMMUNITY-FIRST APPROACH

Understanding needs before action

Geographic expansion is guided by demonstrated need, vulnerability, community readiness, partner availability, funding, operational capacity and programme evidence.

How we understand communities

Community consultations, baseline assessments, household surveys, vulnerability analysis, stakeholder mapping, gender analysis, disability inclusion analysis, environmental assessment and community priority ranking.

Phased reach

Phase 1: Northern Ghana. Phase 2: Ghana-wide replication. Phase 3: international expansion, subject to legal presence, partnerships, qualified personnel, sustainable financing and demonstrated programme capacity.

  • Protection from sexual exploitation and abuse
  • Prevention of sexual harassment
  • Code of conduct
  • Safe recruitment
  • Staff training
  • Incident reporting

ACCOUNTABILITY FRAMEWORK

Responsible stewardship in practice

VIRD is accountable to communities, partners, donors and other stakeholders.

Institutional accountability

Financial and procurement controls, internal oversight, external audit, donor reporting, partner due diligence, risk management and data protection.

Programme accountability

Programme monitoring, beneficiary feedback, complaints mechanisms, safeguarding and Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning.

Community feedback

Communities can raise programme concerns, questions about assistance or beneficiary selection, staff-conduct concerns, safeguarding concerns, fraud or corruption concerns, and suggestions for improvement.

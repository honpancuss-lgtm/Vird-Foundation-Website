CURRENT OPENINGS

VIRD FOUNDATION

Tagline: Transforming Lives Through Faith, Compassion and Sustainable Development.

JOB VACANCIES

Introduction

VIRD Foundation is seeking qualified, highly motivated and results-driven professionals to join our team to support the delivery of our humanitarian, development and Islamic social finance programmes in Northern Ghana and beyond.

PART 1: VACANT POSITIONS

01. HUMAN RESOURCE MANAGER

What the role does: Lead and manage the organisation's human resources functions, including recruitment, staff development, performance management and HR compliance.

02. BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT MANAGER

What the role does: Drive partnership development, resource mobilisation and organisational growth through strategic business development and donor engagement.

03. HUMANITARIAN AND EMERGENCY RESPONSE MANAGER

What the role does: Lead emergency preparedness, response and recovery programmes to support vulnerable communities.

04. MEAL SPECIALIST

What the role does: Design and implement monitoring, evaluation, accountability and learning systems to ensure evidence-based, high-quality programming.

05. WASH SPECIALIST

What the role does: Plan and implement water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH) programmes to improve access to safe water and sanitation services.

06. FOOD AND AGRICULTURE SPECIALIST

What the role does: Support sustainable agriculture, food security and climate-smart agricultural initiatives for resilient livelihoods.

PART 2: VACANT POSITIONS

07. LIVELIHOODS AND ECONOMIC EMPOWERMENT/GEIS SPECIALIST

What the role does: Design and implement livelihoods and economic empowerment programmes with a focus on inclusion and safeguards.

08. ISLAMIC FINANCES AND INFRASTRUCTURE PROGRAMME SPECIALIST

What the role does: Lead Islamic social finance initiatives and support mosque and community infrastructure programmes.

09. CHILD AND SOCIAL PROTECTION SPECIALIST

What the role does: Develop and implement child protection and broader social protection programmes.

10. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT, PEACE BUILDING & SOCIAL COHESION MANAGER

What the role does: Lead community development, peacebuilding and social cohesion initiatives.

11. HEALTH AND NUTRITION SPECIALIST

What the role does: Plan and implement health and nutrition programmes to improve access to quality healthcare and nutrition services.

12. CLIMATE AND ENVIRONMENTAL RESILIENCE SPECIALIST

What the role does: Lead climate adaptation, environmental resilience and natural resource management initiatives.
